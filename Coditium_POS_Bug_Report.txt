==============================================================================
CODITIUM POS - BUG REPORT
Application: https://pos.coditium.com
Test date:   22 Oct 2026 (PKT), pre-production build, dummy data
Prepared by: POS QA
==============================================================================

SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
51 unique defects found: 3 Critical, 12 High, 19 Medium, 17 Low. Role
enforcement, payments and stock integrity need fixing before go-live.

Severity scale
  Critical = security bypass or silent loss of financial/stock data
  High     = wrong records or a blocked core workflow
  Medium   = validation gaps, misleading output or notable risk
  Low      = cosmetic or minor friction

FIX THESE FIRST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BUG-001 Enforce role permissions (a Cashier can open Users, Setup and
   delete products).
2. BUG-002 Make payments update invoice Paid/Balance and reject invalid
   amounts.
3. BUG-003 Stop Monthly Supply from resetting Paid on existing invoices.
4. BUG-004 Block overselling and make stock movements reversible and exact.
5. BUG-005 Clear Credit POS entries once invoiced to prevent double billing.

BUG-001 [Critical] Users/Roles
Role permissions are not enforced; a Cashier can reach every admin page
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steps to reproduce:
  1. Create user QA-cashier with role Cashier and sign in as that user.
  2. Open /Setup, /Users/Create, /AuditLogs, /Configurations, /Branches,
     /PurchaseInvoices, /Inventories, /Expenses, /Reports.
  3. Delete a product (QA-V2) from /Products.
Expected:
  Cashier is limited to POS screens; admin pages return 403 / Access Denied
  and destructive actions are blocked.
Actual:
  Every page returns HTTP 200 and the Cashier deleted product QA-V2. Roles
  Admin, Manager, Cashier and Viewer exist but have no effect.
Page / evidence:
  https://pos.coditium.com/Users/Index, https://pos.coditium.com/Setup (as
  QA-cashier). Round 3 #1.

BUG-002 [Critical] Payments
Customer/vendor payments do not update invoice Paid or Balance and accept
invalid amounts
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steps to reproduce:
  1. Record a customer payment against credit invoice POS-M-20261021212609600.
  2. Open the invoice and check Paid and Balance.
  3. Try amounts 0, -100, 9,999 (customer) and 99,999 (vendor).
Expected:
  Payment is allocated to the invoice, Paid rises and Balance falls; zero,
  negative and overpayments are rejected.
Actual:
  Invoice still shows Paid 0. All four invalid amounts were saved. There is no
  customer or vendor balance or ledger view to reconcile.
Page / evidence:
  https://pos.coditium.com/CustomerPayments,
  https://pos.coditium.com/VendorPayments. Round 3 #6.

BUG-003 [Critical] Monthly Supply
"Add Supplied" resets an existing invoice's Paid amount to 0
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steps to reproduce:
  1. Customer M Shahid has monthly invoice dated 2026-10-01 with Net 6,810 and
     Paid 3,500.
  2. In Monthly Supply, click "Add Supplied" for the customer.
  3. Open the monthly invoice POS-MS-202610-1000.
Expected:
  New lines are appended; Net increases and Paid stays 3,500.
Actual:
  Net became 7,060 and Paid became 0, so a recorded payment of 3,500 was lost.
Page / evidence:
  Monthly Supply page; invoice POS-MS-202610-1000. Round 2 #3.

BUG-004 [High] Inventory/Stock
Overselling allowed; stock silently clamps to 0 and delete restores the wrong
quantity
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steps to reproduce:
  1. In Cash POS add product "Large 270" (stock 0) and pay; also sell QA-P1 at
     zero stock.
  2. Sell 100 x Yougart while stock is 73.
  3. Check inventory, then delete that sale and check again.
Expected:
  Sale is blocked (or allowed only under an explicit negative-stock policy)
  and stock moves by the exact quantity both ways.
Actual:
  All sales saved with no out-of-stock indicator. Yougart dropped to 0 (not
  -27); deleting the sale restored 73, not 100, so the stock ledger no longer
  reconciles.
Page / evidence:
  https://pos.coditium.com/POS/Cash, https://pos.coditium.com/Inventories.
  Recon #13, Round 2 #1, Round 3 #9.
